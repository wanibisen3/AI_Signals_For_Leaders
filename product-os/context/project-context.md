# Project Context: AI Signals for Leaders

## Product Name
AI Signals for Leaders

## Product Summary
AI Signals for Leaders is a web app that helps professionals understand how AI developments affect their industry, function, company context, and career relevance.

## Core User Problem
Most professionals and leaders see a flood of generic AI news but struggle to answer:
- What matters to me?
- How does this affect my role, industry, or company?
- What should I pay attention to right now?

## Core Value Proposition
The product turns broad AI news and developments into personalized, relevant signals for a user’s context.

## Target Users
- Managers
- Product leaders
- Strategy professionals
- Operators
- Business professionals
- Founders
- Knowledge workers who need curated AI relevance, not raw news

## Current Product Direction
The app provides personalized AI briefings based on user personalization inputs such as:
- role
- company maturity
- main focus
- keywords / interests

Users can also skip personalization and see major AI updates only.

## Key Product Capabilities
- AI news / signal aggregation
- personalized briefing generation
- dashboard experience
- personalization editing
- token or usage-based system
- possibly monetized plans / subscriptions

## Product Principles
- Relevance over volume
- Personalization over generic news
- Clear signal over noisy summaries
- Fast value for busy professionals
- Simple UX, not enterprise complexity

## Business Goal
Build a real product that people use regularly and may pay for, while showcasing strong product thinking and AI-native execution.

## Current Constraints
- MVP mindset
- avoid overbuilding
- focus on useful, narrow functionality
- prioritize user value and repeat usage
- page refresh should not unnecessarily trigger expensive generation
- existing personalization and briefing logic should feel intentional and efficient

## What Good Looks Like
- a user quickly understands why a given AI update matters
- personalized briefings feel tailored, not generic
- the product is lightweight, credible, and habit-forming
- features support retention and monetization, not just novelty
